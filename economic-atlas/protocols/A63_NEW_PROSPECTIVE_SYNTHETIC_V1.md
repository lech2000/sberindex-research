# A6.3: prospective validation design, 8 October 2026

This design does not repair the old preregistration. Action act_63eabe3fd8aa4770 promised an error budget before the control run. The saved 3 October protocol fixed worlds, seeds and thresholds but no quantitative acceptance tolerance. Its 210 configurations, 4,830 postinitial month rows and 348 predicted event rows therefore remain INCONCLUSIVE_NO_PRIOR_ERROR_BUDGET. New M2/M3 results use different thresholds and geometries and cannot substitute for that promise.

A new explicitly prospective amendment can supply the missing type of evidence on fresh random realizations. It cannot make “before the old run” true. Closing the unchanged historical promise as satisfied would be inaccurate. Any eventual case closeout must say that the historical requirement failed and that a separately frozen prospective validation completed, with its actual negative or positive limited verdict. Completion is not conditional on finding PASS.

## Full preserved scope

Keep the SHA-pinned `a6_frozen_controls.world` generator and original `a6_identities` tracker, calibrations and evaluator. Do not substitute the anisotropic geometry-v3 or geometry-v404 generators. Five new SHA-derived seed keys cover all seven original classes, 35 cubes of 200 points × 24 months × 5 dimensions, both oracle and unsupervised modes, and all three M factors 0.75/1/1.25. This yields 70 label histories, 210 configurations and exactly 4,830 postinitial month comparisons. Fifty truth events imply 300 configuration-level truth expectations. The old 348 prediction rows are not a required future prediction count: save every new prediction, including duplicates and false events.

The original thresholds remain T=0.27716275979336036, M=0.7187007760980421, Tc=0.17395474720266485. All three M factors are prespecified sensitivities; each of all six mode × factor cells must satisfy its frozen budget for an overall limited suite PASS. No passing cell can be selected as the final method. Recognition FDR/FNR ≤5%, known-pair FPR ≤1%, coverage ≥95%, event FDR/FNR ≤10%, and zero false split/merge events in stable/drift/proximity are prospective engineering tolerances. They are not estimates of performance in real municipalities. Old sensitivity failures were already seen and are openly disclosed; this is fresh-seed confirmation within a known ontology, not blind discovery of world classes.

## Execution architecture requiring subsequent implementation

The four-file candidate is a design freeze and stdlib acceptance component, **not a launchable scientific runner**. A later reviewed runner must import the pinned old generator and evaluator normally, verify their byte SHA first, pass fresh seeds as explicit arguments without changing module globals, and keep thresholds literal. One cube at a time: generate and save cube/truth hashes; compute the two label histories once; then reuse those histories for all three factors. Preserve all 6 M3 geometry sensitivities per configuration, not just operating-point events. Original source parameters remain K=2..8 and n_init=10: 5,880 K-selection fits plus 840 selected-K fits, 210 tracking and full-grid M3 calls. No retained best-M or result-dependent seed replacement is allowed.

Save compressed complete assignments/regions/labels, run and month counts, all events, all missing/error statuses, truth matching keys, cube/label hashes and configuration/source association journals. Stream or process one world at a time to respect the bounded output/RSS; compression does not reduce scientific scope. The original scoring retains one-time identity binding, ≥0.8 purity AND coverage, exact month/kind/subject/parent-child matching, duplicate predictions as FP, unknown predictions in FDR, and abstentions as FN where truth matches exist. Event FPR stays undefined because no TN universe was declared. Add independent reconciliation of all saved rows and raw scoring, including all sensitivity cells, before any gate.

A future one-use guardian must anchor its inclusive 1,800-second allowance before imports, verify original pins and new protocol/root binding, enforce CPU1/RSS1GiB/free1GiB/output128MiB and 30-second receipt reserve, and supervise only its own process group. Do not launch in this design. Native preflight, actual environment pinning, source adoption and one canonical UID-private O_EXCL lease belong to that later accepted runner. No retry/resume or seed replacement after inference. Resource STOP retains the whole requested registry and yields INCONCLUSIVE, not a smaller suite PASS. Old timestamp difference ~86.56 seconds is a rough cost observation, not a transferable throughput bound; 1,800 seconds is a bounded allowance rather than a finish guarantee.

## Mathematical and interpretive checks

Read-only source inspection confirms original labels use dtype=object: A1/A2 are not silently truncated. Original split/merge changes geometry (unlike a separate later constant-coordinate label world). The death control uses an explicit four-miss retirement convention; the previously observed month mismatch remains an error under exact matching, not a reason to redefine truth. Proximity is a no-event negative world. An isolated/inconclusive clustering state cannot be dropped to improve a rate. Generator formulas are retained, not fit to the new seeds. This design has not generated the new cubes or examined their accuracy.

Independent units are five seed blocks, not 4,830 independent months. Paired 10,000-block bootstrap intervals are descriptive and may be degenerate; they do not support zero population error. A limited synthetic PASS, if observed, is neither external real-world validation nor causal/economic identity PASS, and does not undo historical lack of an error budget. M1 failure and all other gates remain separate evidence.
