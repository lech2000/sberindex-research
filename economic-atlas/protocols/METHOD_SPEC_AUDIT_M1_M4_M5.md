## Prospective choices that the original M1 leaves unspecified

M1_PROSPECTIVE_V1.json is frozen before method experiments. Tau_lambda=.95; full-tail population standard deviation; epsilon1e-12 yields undefined sig instead of an infinite artifact. Graph is symmetric, binary, sparsified cosine kNN: union directedkNN candidates sorted deterministically by cosine then indices, retain floor(n*k/2) edges. Thus controls and real inputs have **exact identical undirected density** for equal n,k. This pruning is explicit prospective interpretation of unspecified symmetrization, not a historical A5 graph or ordinary unpruned union. It can yield isolates; they are INCONCLUSIVE and are never bridged. Original candidate graph construction is not claimed uniquely determined by the source. No simpler time-average or fixed-k method replaces M1.

Positives R3/4/5 and negatives are generated in five shares with the same n,d,k and density. Controls sum to .72 as a fixed chosen synthetic convention; real shares are never forcibly normalized. Calibration seeds10 and held seeds20 per world/k are disjoint. Sig* uses strict full calibration extrema, no ATF7.3 import and no quantile fallback. Positive calibration requires exact R; missing separation is retained. Held error budget: accepted structure on negative worlds <=5%; exact R with REAL_GAP on positives >=90%, each k. This M1 budget measures **accepted** false structure; it is not the stronger M4 requirement of raw m1 on every foreign-channel world. All raw m values are preserved.

Mandatory null independently permutes columns, preserving each marginal and destroying cross-feature combinations; common row permutation would merely rename vertices and is not used. Each null uses its own m for raw gap, strict realgap>empirical p95, with99 samples. Missing/isolated null graphs cause INCONCLUSIVE_SHUFFLE. This is per-graph descriptive p95, not familywise evidence across72 graphs or an independent economic holdout. K2..8 is a fixed comparator grid; spectral m is added even outside it, never replaced by silhouette optimum. SW/CH and accepted pair-union S_Dbw are evaluated on the same raw monthly shares.

CLI freezes code/protocol/input/calibration SHA and refuses existing outputs or altered protocol. Each new real calculation reads frozen panel and exact A5 mask, not old labels. It reports all calibration/held data, every graph/month, raw gaps and invalid reasons. If held controls fail, real diagnostics may still be replayed, but REAL_GAP is downgraded to INCONCLUSIVE_CONTROL_QUALITY. Economic identity, causal PASS and independent holdout are always false.
